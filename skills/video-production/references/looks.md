# Named looks

Use when a brief has no brand or asks for a style, so the video has a clear point of view instead of a default. Pick one, name it to the user, and adapt it to supplied brand assets. Each is an untested starting point: render a short moving test and judge it before scaling. Install or license fonts first; the names below are examples with open licenses, and any close substitute works.

| Look | Suits | Type | Colour | Motion | Sound |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Field Notes** | Founder stories, explainers | Serif headlines (Fraunces), plain sans body | Warm paper, ink, one muted accent | Slow pushes, content eases in over 300–500 ms | Soft felt piano, room tone |
| **Control Room** | Developer tools, data products | Mono labels (JetBrains Mono), tight sans | Near-black, one signal colour, dim grid | Quick snaps, numbers count up, UI zooms | Low pulse, small clicks |
| **Poster** | Launches, ads | Heavy grotesque at huge sizes (Anton) | Two flat colours at full contrast | Hard cuts on the beat, words as images | Punchy drums, hits on cuts |
| **Studio Clean** | Product demos, SaaS | Neutral sans (Inter) | White, soft grey, brand colour for action only | Calm zooms to the action, cursor leads the eye | Light ambient bed, quiet ticks |
| **Handheld** | Testimonials, behind the scenes | Clean sans captions, minimal titles | Natural footage, light grade only | Real camera movement, jump cuts, punch-ins | Location sound, little music |
| **Chalkboard** | Teaching, maths, science | Rounded sans or a clear hand style | Dark slate, chalk colours for meaning | Diagrams build step by step | Voice first, near silence |
| **Newsprint** | Data stories, reports | Serif headlines, compact sans labels | Off-white, black, one highlight | Charts draw on, highlights sweep across text | Light percussion, paper textures |
| **Neon Night** | Gaming, music, nightlife | Wide display face | Deep navy or black, saturated glow | Fast cuts, glitches used sparingly | Synth bass, risers |

Keep one look per video, and keep body text and captions legible whatever the look. Accessibility wins over style: check contrast at delivery size, and avoid rapid full-screen flashes.
